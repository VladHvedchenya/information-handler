At dusk, Mara reached the abandoned lighthouse. Inside her bag, she carried a map, a rope, and 13 silver coins. A brass key hung beside the entrance. Could the key open the wooden door?

The key turned slowly in the rusty lock. Behind the door, a narrow staircase disappeared into darkness. Suddenly, a warning bell rang above her!

Mara followed the map through a tunnel beneath the lighthouse. The map showed a boat, but the boat was gone. As the sunrise touched the water, she spotted the boat drifting toward a distant island.
